# Limitations & Future Scope

---

## Limitations

- Accuracy can drop when faces are not clearly visible during initial identification  
- Limited dataset size may affect generalization  
- Real-time performance depends on hardware capability  

Once an identity is confidently assigned, the system continues tracking even if the face is no longer visible. However, incorrect early predictions can still propagate.

---

## Future Scope

- Improve robustness under extreme occlusion and motion  
- Evaluate on larger and more diverse datasets  
- Optimize for faster real-time deployment
